Clarifications & Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Find fast answers on battery longevity, extreme winter durability, and sorting accuracy.

Each EcoBin One integrates an aerospace-grade monocrystalline solar roof paired with a high-capacity LiFePO4 battery pack designed to retain 120 hours (5 full days) of operational reserve. Furthermore, our edge AI hardware operates in ultra-low power standby (0.4W) between user interactions.

EcoBin features hermetically sealed magnetic chute flaps that block all external access. After waste is deposited, an internal chamber misting mechanism distributes a 100% bio-based enzymatic odor neutralizer alongside an automated UV-C sterilization cycle.

Our optical sensor classifies items into four categories: recyclables, compost, e-waste, and general landfill residue. If an item cannot be definitively identified or contains high food contamination, the bin gracefully defaults to the non-recyclable residual compartment, preventing cross-contamination of clean recyclable feedstock.

No special trucks are required! EcoPulse Cloud generates dynamic daily routes exported directly as GPX/KML turn-by-turn navigation sent to your existing driver tablets or smartphones. The software ensures drivers only visit bins that are 80%+ full.

Strictly privacy-preserving: EcoBin does not store facial images. Optical sensors face downward into the sorting tray only. The GreenRewards app uses zero-knowledge cryptography to award reward tokens without tracking personal biometric profiles.
